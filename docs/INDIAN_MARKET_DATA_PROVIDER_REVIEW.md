# Indian equity market-data provider review

**As of 7 November 2026. Documentation-only discovery.** No authenticated API call, purchase, dataset download, credential use, ingestion, model training, or model promotion was performed. Rankings below assess *pilot suitability*, not a grant of data rights or a claim that an API works in this project. Prices and product terms can change; obtain a written quote and licence before paying or ingesting.

## Recommendation

**Primary pilot candidate: DhanHQ Data API, conditional on written rights and account access.** Its [historical-data documentation](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/historical-data/) describes daily OHLCV from a security's inception and 1/5/15-minute OHLCV for up to five years on active instruments, with at most 90 days of intraday data per request. Its [instrument master](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/instruments/) includes security ID, exchange, ISIN and series. Dhan [states](https://dhan.co/support/platforms/dhanhq-api/is-the-historical-data-from-dhan-s-data-api-adjusted-for-corporate-actions-like-bonuses-and-splits/) that daily data is adjusted for bonuses and splits. [Published subscription cost](https://dhan.co/support/platforms/dhanhq-api/how-does-the-dhanhq-data-api-subscription-work/) is **₹499 plus applicable taxes per 30 days**, via a Dhan account. The [API introduction](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/) lists Data API limits of 5 requests/second and 100,000/day. Daily adjusted prices must **not** be assumed point-in-time safe: adjustments applied after a future split can alter earlier bars. The adjustment version, ex-date history and access to delisted equities are unverified.

**Secondary validation source: directly licensed NSE Data & Analytics for NSE, and BSE information products for BSE.** NSE publishes a [data-use policy](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/nse-data-policy) requiring a relevant agreement governing intended use, handling and dissemination. Its [EOD/historical product page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/eod-historical-data-subscription) describes CM EOD and historical trade/order products, with a contact for subscription. The [April 2026 domestic tariff](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/mediaattachment/2026-04/Download_Pricing_file_-_Domestic_clients_20260424122229.pdf) lists **₹1,00,000/year** for CM EOD at a site and **₹1,10,000/year** for CM historical *trade* data, exclusive of taxes. These are distinct products and published tariff categories, **not a quote for five years of research-ready daily candles or ML rights**. BSE's [market-data portal](https://marketdata.bseindia.com/) lists equity, corporate and index OHLC products with registration, KYC, plan selection, agreement and payment; pricing for this use needs a quote. Existing five NSE ZIPs are useful comparison observations, but their source terms have not been certified for broader research use.

**Decision gate:** If Dhan will not explicitly permit the proposed storage, local backtesting, ML training, derived features/model artifacts and intended future commercial use, seek a direct NSE/BSE licence or another vendor agreement. Do not treat a ₹499 API subscription, a free broker token or a public download as permission for those uses.

**Reliability and completeness:** The cited pages establish advertised capabilities, not measured uptime, complete historical sessions, correct ISIN continuity, raw/adjusted versioning or a data-quality SLA. None has been independently tested through this project, apart from the documented Upstox 403 denials. Missing market sessions must be distinguished from exchange holidays, halts and vendor omissions using an authoritative calendar and source logs. A candle timestamp alone does not establish when that candle first became available to a researcher.

## Rights and technical questions to send before purchase

Ask Dhan, NSE Data & Analytics and any backup vendor for written answers covering: (1) private local storage and backups, retention after subscription ends, export and cross-provider comparison; (2) historical backtests, feature generation and ML model training, including retention of derived features, trained artifacts and results; (3) future commercial application use and any exchange/non-display licence; (4) raw versus adjusted daily/minute data, split/bonus/dividend methodology, historical adjustment vintages and announcement/ex-date timestamps; (5) delisted stocks, ticker/ISIN changes, historical universe or constituent lists; (6) index OHLCV and benchmark licensing; (7) authoritative NSE/BSE holidays, special sessions, halts and first-publication or correction timestamps; (8) correction/revision policy, completeness guarantees, support and sample-data terms. The [NSE policy](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/nse-data-policy) makes the use-specific agreement decisive; broker API access by itself is not that agreement.

## Bounded acquisition plan, subject to approval and rights

1. Obtain the written rights and a dated quote; preserve their scope outside public Git. Do not buy or request credentials until the user approves the chosen plan.
2. Freeze a five-stock **NSE EQ** pilot universe: RELIANCE, TCS, INFY, HDFCBANK and ICICIBANK. Verify exchange, series, ISIN, provider security ID and mapping validity for each. Use only **26–30 October 2026** daily bars for first comparison, matching the five existing NSE ZIP dates. Record the provider response and retrieval time separately from unproven first-available time. Use a disposable database and preserve raw/source and adjusted status; never overwrite canonical NSE rows on conflict.
3. Reconcile each overlapping instrument/date for open, high, low, close and volume in integer paise, with exact difference and both sources' identifiers. Classify exact match, documented acceptable difference, or conflict; choose no silent tolerance. Investigate corporate actions, auction/close conventions and corrections before accepting a divergence. Run existing point-in-time/data-readiness gates; do not train on this five-day pilot.
4. **Daily benchmarking threshold (proposed, not a profitability guarantee):** after rights and quality are verified, seek approximately **five years of daily data for at least 100 historically identified NSE EQ instruments**, including symbols that delisted or left the universe, plus an authorised index benchmark and corporate-action events. Use a universe fixed as of a historical date, explicit replacements/delistings, a chronological development/validation period and at least 252 trading sessions in a sealed final test period. Prefer more history and breadth if licensing permits. A 100-stock current-survivors-only sample is **not** sufficient to clear survivorship risk. Retrospectively adjusted series must not enter historical features without an as-of-safe adjustment policy.
5. **Separate intraday path:** only after daily rights and pilot quality are established, request a small licensed 1-minute and 5-minute sample for two mapped equities over one month, with verified session/holiday/halt grids, completion and first-availability semantics, revisions, spread/liquidity limitations and explicit exchange identity. Expand to multiple years and more symbols only after a separate approval. Do not infer realistic execution from OHLCV alone.

The existing nine-model infrastructure and any future Champion–Challenger governance stay unchanged. The pilot is a data-quality and licensing exercise, not a model-training permit. No real-data model is trained, selected or promoted until the readiness gate passes and a separate training plan is approved.
